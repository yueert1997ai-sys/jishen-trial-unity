# P0.3 audio revision

Version: `p0.3-energy-rifle-audio-20260909`. Same local executable and desktop launcher as P0.2.

The rejected Subspace BGM is replaced in P0 by MintoDog's Heavy Battle 2, with its complete 83.027-second / 64-bar loop. A short boundary fade avoids a click. Loop playback replaces the arbitrary 2.5-second crossfade, which would offset the beat. Music ducks quickly during rifle fire and sword contact; existing music/effects preferences are preserved.

Sword audio now has original electrical FM/harmonic sweeps and plasma contact layers instead of the earlier broadband air swishes. Fast strokes last 220 / 190 ms; the heavy stroke has a 380 ms tail. Existing animation-trigger timing, three discrete cuts, one heavy preparation and no empty-swing hit sounds are preserved.

M7 has three recorded-rifle variants based on a CC0 .223/M4 source, isolated so one gameplay event cannot play an entire recorded burst. A short body, crack and diffuse tail replace the previous explosion/noise construction. The player's gun voice is louder relative to BGM and uses only 1% random pitch variation. Ballistics, damage, fire interval and controls are unchanged.

References were decoded and measured offline: the Gundam clip has concentrated mid/high-frequency energy, while the M7 demonstration has a strong low-frequency report. No reference recording is sampled into runtime audio. See [source licenses](licenses/P0_Audio_V3.md).

## Validation and limits

- `tools/p0/verify_audio_v3.py`: all ten PCM clips pass peak, DC and boundary checks; complete loop duration and seam pass; a representative unducked gun/combo/music mix peaks at -1.54 dBFS.
- `tools/p0/run_p0.py build`: strict Unity standalone build.
- `tools/p0/run_p0.py check`: new audio bank loads, cue counts remain correct, new loop binds to the mixer and rejected BGM is absent. Also runs existing ballistics, combo, dash and five-minute simulated arena checks.
- Evidence: `AuditEvidence/p0-audio-v3/` and `AuditEvidence/p0-combat/`.
- No Computer Use, visible playtest, audio-device playback or subjective acoustic audition. The five-minute regression is invulnerable fixed-time simulation, not a natural human playthrough. `offline_mix_not_runtime_capture.wav` is an offline mix check, not recorded gameplay audio.
