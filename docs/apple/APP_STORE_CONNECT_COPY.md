## Beta App Description / 简体中文

《机神试炼》是一款俯视视角机甲动作游戏。选择机体，搭配远程射击、近战斩击、推进闪避和机体技能，挑战敌方机甲与首领，并在战斗流程中回收装备。本次 iPhone 测试重点验证触屏操作、战斗反馈、关卡流程及设备运行表现。

## Beta App Description / English

Mech Trial is a top-down mech action game. Choose a mech and combine ranged fire, melee attacks, boost dodges, and mech abilities against enemies and bosses. Recover equipment as you progress. This iPhone beta focuses on touch controls, combat feedback, encounter flow, and device performance.

## What to Test / 简体中文

请横屏游玩。左侧摇杆移动，右侧摇杆瞄准并射击；点击斩击、推进和机体技能按钮，测试移动与攻击同时操作的表现。

请重点反馈：
1. 连续触摸、多指操作时是否失灵、卡住或误触。
2. 射击、斩击、闪避和机体技能的反馈是否清楚。
3. 机库选择、进入战斗、暂停、失败重试、结算和继续关卡是否正常。
4. 锁屏或切换应用后返回，是否有无声、卡住或自动移动。
5. 持续游玩后的发热、掉帧、闪退，以及刘海/灵动岛是否遮挡按钮。

提交反馈时请注明 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、构建号、发生场景和复现步骤；可附截图或短录屏。

## What to Test / English

Play in landscape. Move with the left stick; aim and fire with the right stick. Try melee, boost, and mech abilities while moving. Please report missed or stuck touch input, combat readability, progression and retry problems, issues after returning from the background, crashes, sustained performance, heat, and controls obscured by the safe area. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, build number, and reproduction steps.

## Beta Review Notes / English — verify on iPhone before submission

This is an early iPhone gameplay beta. No sign-in or purchase is required in the current local implementation. Launch the app, select a mech in the hangar, then use the on-screen deployment control to enter combat. Use the left virtual stick to move and the right virtual stick to aim and fire. On-screen buttons control melee, boost, mech abilities, and pause. Please review the attached device-tested build; final button labels and screenshots should be verified against that build before submitting these notes.
