# P0.10 M7 refinement — 2026-09-11

Active project: D:/project-mecha-design/MECH ROUGE/handoff/GameplayLoop_V1, branch codex/equipment-loop-v1.

The sand-colored M7 now has separate desert coating, brighter beveled edge material, dark metal hardware and rubber/polymer surface response. A derivative-filtered procedural coating shader restores fine surface variation lost by the original color-only Blender export. Four receiver fasteners with recessed sockets and a receiver-side EjectionPort are added to the existing Unity prefab; the authored M7 FBX, stock, scope, grip and muzzle alignment are retained. Original materials remain available; the refined copies are scoped to M7 in Assets/Art/M7Refined.

M7 fires a revolved pointed copper mesh at the existing speed of 72, with a 0.018-second tracer, a short combustion flash and light local barrel smoke. Damage, fire cadence, spread and armor-break values are unchanged. This update strengthens physical presentation rather than changing weapon balance.

Each emitted round ejects a hollow-neck brass case from the receiver. Cases rotate with speed, fall under gravity, bounce, and persist for 8–10 seconds before shrinking out. The particle budget is capped at 160. World collision handles solid static scenery; a cosmetic y=0.055 floor catch handles the P0 flat arena, which has no reliable floor collider for particle collision. It adds no gameplay collider. Pause freezes the particle systems; leaving combat clears them. The fixed buffer adds no per-frame particle-array allocation.
