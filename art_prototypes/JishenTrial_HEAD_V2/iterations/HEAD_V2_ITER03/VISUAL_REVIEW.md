- TOP / ANTENNA ROOT: roof now follows the central raised station, and two short fins remain coherent.
- FOREHEAD: the newly added inner cranium crosses the outer forehead plane and produces a wide raised band. It must be clamped behind the analytic plane of the actual armor, with positive clearance.
- EYE LINE: the old floating rim is gone, but the larger orbital bed still projects in front of the cheek/mask and reads as an oversized eye plate. Recede its lower and outer surfaces into the face; do not change only the optic depth.
- CHEEK WIDTH: outer cheek is now a clear thin plane and the underlying chassis tapers instead of ending in square blocks.
- MASK TIP / CHIN: compact proportions retained. The chin's rear cradle needs to reach the inner side supports so it does not appear suspended in profile.
- REAR HEAD: angular profile and inner support are readable; keep primary geometry simple.
- NECK: the continuous octagonal collar now surrounds the yaw ring. Raise its base slightly to shorten the exposed neck support.
