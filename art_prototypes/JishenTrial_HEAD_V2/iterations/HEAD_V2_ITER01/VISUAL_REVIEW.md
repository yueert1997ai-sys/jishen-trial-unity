# ITER01 — actual review, rejected for further primary-form correction

Reopened HEAD_V2_ITER01.blend and rendered HEAD_FRONT, HEAD_SIDE, HEAD_3Q at 1440² in Blender 5.2.1. All three images were actually opened and inspected against the supplied HEAD DETAIL and its small front/side drawings.

- TOP: angular crest replaces the rejected spherical cap, but the top-side return has a visible open gap. Close that structural gap.
- ANTENNA ROOT: now exactly two coherent fins; their silhouette is still taller than the requested short fins. Reduce the tip height.
- FOREHEAD: connected front/temple panel contains irregular folds because the control points do not share a plane. Replace them with a deliberate planar face and a roof return.
- EYE LINE: short optics are present, but their surrounding rim still reads as an external pair of glasses. Move the entire housing behind the brow, reduce its visible lip, and keep the optic deeper still.
- CHEEK WIDTH: tapered outline is better; inner cheek-bearing mesh produces a large folded pocket in side view. Rebuild its support as a closed planar structure.
- MASK TIP / CHIN: lower face is too long compared to the concept. Shorten it from the eye-line datum and narrow the mask; retain the independent ridge and chin.
- REAR HEAD: rear panel is angular, but its connection to the temporal panel has an excessive opening. Extend the existing panel connection, not extra detail.

Gray material is physically neutral but ITER01 light energy makes the exposed faces very pale. Reduce all three light powers together for legibility, retaining their fixed positions and sizes. No attractive material treatment or secondary detailing is permitted.
