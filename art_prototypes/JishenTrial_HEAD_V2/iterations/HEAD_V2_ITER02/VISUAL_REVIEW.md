# ITER02 — actual front, side and 3Q review

All three saved 1440² Blender renders were opened and compared to the concept. This iteration is retained, but is not the proposed acceptance model.

- TOP: main roof opening is smaller; a triangular gap remains because the temporal upper edge does not follow the crown's raised middle station. Add that missing shared station, not decorative cover plates.
- ANTENNA ROOT: coherent pair, shorter silhouette retained.
- FOREHEAD: large planar face is much cleaner. Central backing is still too empty; close it with a faceted internal cranium that supports the separate outer panels.
- EYE LINE: moving the little rim inward did not remove the glasses silhouette. Replace its narrow external contour with a full facial orbital bed; keep a small recessed aperture inside it. This is a structural rebuild of the same housing, not a detail pass.
- CHEEK WIDTH: outer contour is acceptable at this stage; inner chassis still has a wide square-ended lower silhouette. Taper its solid support and make the thin outer cheek plate planar.
- MASK TIP / CHIN: 26% shorter lower face and 10% narrower mask remove the long muzzle. Keep those proportions for the next comparison.
- REAR HEAD: angular rear profile retained. The added cranium backs the remaining gaps.
- NECK: the forward collar piece still looks detached in side view. Replace its shape with a low octagonal armor collar surrounding the yaw ring and touching the twin pitch supports.

No bolts, vents, panel lines, textures or emission were added. Camera transforms and projection remain unchanged.
