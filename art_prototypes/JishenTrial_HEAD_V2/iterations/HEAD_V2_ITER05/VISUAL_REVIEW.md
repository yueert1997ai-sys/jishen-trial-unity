# ITER05 — primary-form review candidate

The FRONT, SIDE and 3Q images were opened and compared to the supplied concept. The crown/chin-aligned comparison was also regenerated. The major widths now follow the front reference more closely; fin roots move inward; the face mask ends higher and leaves a separate jaw instead of one long muzzle. The same orthographic cameras were retained.

- TOP: narrow central crown and explicit planar side returns; no large spherical cap or remaining open roof gap.
- ANTENNA ROOT: two mirrored roots, moved inward; deliberately shorter fins than the original multi-fin concept, following the new brief.
- FOREHEAD: independent central wedge and broad side armor, supported by an inset faceted cranium. No raised internal strip crossing the outside forehead.
- EYE LINE: short straight optics behind brow/cavity; all gray. Eye-line ray probes hit the intended independent optics, not a protruding front casing.
- CHEEK WIDTH: overall width reduced by 14% based on actual aligned comparison, with outer thin plate and internal support remaining separate.
- MASK TIP: height shortened 22% about the upper connection line; the tip no longer extends almost to the chin.
- CHIN: separate angular cradle, upper edge raised to meet the shortened mask's underside, attached back into the inner cheek-bearing structure.
- REAR HEAD: faceted rear shield and nape return remain deliberately simple. Reference secondary layering is not claimed complete.

Final mechanical check keeps circular cross-sections on both neck axes after the width adjustment. The first ITER05 images are archived before that limited radius correction.

Stop at primary-form review. No material styling, emission, texture, bolts, vents, panel lines, greebles, body work, rigging or integration is authorized as an automatic next stage.
